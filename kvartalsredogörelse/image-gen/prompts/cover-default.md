# Default cover prompt — Sonetel Kvartalsredogörelse

Use as the starting point for a quarterly cover. Vary the setting and nationality across quarters so the cover library reflects Sonetel's 170-country reach over time.

## Setting rotation (suggested cadence)

| Quarter | Suggested setting |
|---|---|
| Q1 (jan-mar) | Bangalore co-working courtyard at golden hour |
| Q2 (apr-jun) | Lisbon café street with azulejo tile walls, late afternoon |
| Q3 (jul-sep) | Marrakech rooftop at golden hour |
| Q4 (oct-dec) | Buenos Aires balcony at dusk, city lights starting |

(Cadence is a suggestion, not a rule — Henrik picks.)

## Q1 jan-mar 2026 default

```
Bangalore co-working courtyard at golden hour. Warm available light, long shadows
across weathered teak floorboards. A South Indian woman in her late thirties is
seated at a low wooden table in the middle distance, a phone resting on the table
beside her notebook, looking out toward a row of arched windows. Plants in
terracotta pots line the courtyard edge; soft late-afternoon light filters through
a high jali screen casting geometric patterns on the wall. Vertical composition
with a clearer lower third for caption space. No text, no signage, no logos.
Shot wide on a 28mm lens at f/4. Available light only. Documentary editorial style.
```

## Q3 jul-sep 2025 default (used for regression-fixture cover)

```
Marrakech rooftop at golden hour. Warm late-afternoon light, long shadows across
weathered terracotta tiles. A Moroccan man in his forties stands at the rooftop
edge in the upper-mid frame, small in the composition, a phone in one hand,
looking toward the distant medina. Stepped rooftops descend behind him, the
Atlas mountains hazy on the horizon. Soft amber light, the call to prayer
implied in the atmosphere but not heard. Vertical composition with a clearer
lower third for caption space. No text, no signage, no logos.
Shot wide on a 28mm lens at f/4. Available light only. Documentary editorial style.
```

## How to vary across quarters

Pick a different city, a different nationality+age, a different time of day. Keep:

- Mid-distance to wide framing
- Editorial documentary feel
- Clear lower-third
- 28mm/35mm at f/4–5.6
- No text/signage
- Sonetel's global reach implied
