  the change explained only 4,300 — the rest was actual revenue for 2026-08-18 landing above run-rate.
  Also caught a 1,376 trough move I had predicted would be ~70 kr.

  the trough is measured at the *end* of day 28 by which point continuous netting has deducted 28/30 of it.
