## Two habits that caught real errors

- **Re-read the trough after every change.** Both wrong predictions in this sweep — the broken offset on id 48
  and the net-zero mirror on id 47/60 — were caught only this way, and both had already been asserted as fact
  in an append-only log entry.
- **When the trough moves more than your change explains, check `cogs_info`, `run_rate_source` and
  `business_parameters` before assuming it was you.** A deploy restored a deleted parameter once; the CEO
  cleared another mid-session.
