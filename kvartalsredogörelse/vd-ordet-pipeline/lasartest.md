# Läsartest — standardsteg efter varje utkast som visas för Henrik (Henrik 2026-11-18)

Kör som en Agent (general-purpose). Ge den: (1) texten på sidan före brevet (sammanfattningen), (2) utkastet. Prompten nedan används ordagrant, med sökvägarna ifyllda.

---
You are running a reader test of a Swedish CEO letter (VD-ord) for a small listed company. Do not edit any files. Read: <SAMMANFATTNING> (the page before the letter; the reader has seen it) and <UTKAST> (the letter).

Read the letter three times, once as each reader, staying in character:
A. Lena, 52, private saver on Avanza, has never heard of Sonetel.
B. Johan, 38, experienced small-cap investor, looks for credibility and red flags.
C. Maria, 61, shareholder for several years, knows the company somewhat.

For each reader, briefly: (1) what is this company and what happened this quarter, in their own words; (2) unclear, confusing or disconnected passages, quoted; (3) coherent story or list of facts; (4) confident, trustworthy, positioned for growth — why/why not; (5) what was missing, or what felt like spin or apology.

Do not score language style or word choice (humans judge Swedish style). Focus on comprehension, coherence and trust. End with a 5-line summary of issues the readers share. Max 700 words.
---

Spara svaret i `<period>/vd-ordet/critics/lasartest-v<N>.md`. Visa Henrik de gemensamma problemen tillsammans med utkastet. Läsarnas önskemål är underlag, inte order: de får aldrig upphäva regeln att inte upprepa sammanfattningens siffror eller förbudet mot framtida finansiella löften.
