## Open — India (~37 % of the model), awaiting Prashant

Asked 2026-08-19. **Prashant pushed back on id 55**: 3,085,000 INR/month labelled "statutory TDS+RTDS" is
implausible as TDS — it would be 77 % of the salary tranche. **He is right, and the label is the error.**
The three tranches are a *percentage split* of one total — 8 % / 40 % / 52 % of **7,719,810 INR/month**,
attributed to Padma April 2026 — not three independently derived amounts. Questions put to him: is the
**total** right, what does the split actually fund, and are annual events (Goa) inside the flat tranches.

**The Swedish side DOES bound the total — an earlier claim in this sweep that it could not was wrong.**
Account 6561 records India cost at only 316,916 SEK/month against 768,445 transferred. The difference is
**capitalised R&D**, which the CEO confirms is booked in two piles: accounts **1011** (350,915/mo) and
**1012** (391,025/mo), 741,940/mo of additions combined. The transfer reconciles as 316,916 expensed +
~451,529 capitalised, and 451,529 fits comfortably inside 741,940 — the remainder being Swedish R&D.
India's exact share of 1011/1012 cannot be isolated without the split, so this **bounds** the total rather
than confirming it. Prashant is still needed for the *composition*.

Related, and relevant to any board discussion: amortisation of capitalised R&D runs ~634,182/month on
account 1019 alone. A large part of India spend never appears as cost in the year it is paid, which is why
the P&L and the cash forecast diverge so sharply.

## Habits that caught real errors

- **Re-read the trough after every change, and explain the whole move.** Day two: a 10,734 improvement where
  the change explained only 4,300 — the rest was actual revenue for 2026-08-18 landing above run-rate.
  Also caught a 1,376 trough move I had predicted would be ~70 kr.
- **The supplier ledger is authoritative; the accrual account is not.** Three times in one day an accrual
  pointed the wrong way — Forvis, Cision, Mangold. See SKILL.md.
- **Check what the trough is measured *at*.** The "66,000 of day-28 distortion" was really 4,400, because
  the trough is measured at the *end* of day 28 by which point continuous netting has deducted 28/30 of it.
